From a Screened Porch to a Four-Season Sunroom with A/C

Location
Longwood, FL
Size
520 sq ft
Investment rangeInvestment ≈

What makes this one different

This wasn’t a standard porch enclosure. The lot needed a retaining wall before anything else could go up. The roof is a flat metal roof with mechanical standing seams, built to handle Florida rain. From the first drawings the ceiling was designed high, so the room feels like part of the house and not an add-on. And a walnut accent wall gives it a finish you don’t expect from a sunroom.

The story behind this project

A wonderful Longwood family had a screened porch they loved — and never used. Too hot in summer, bugs year-round, and every heavy rain sent water down from the upper yard and across the floor.

We designed the fix from the ground up: a retaining wall to stop the water, a real room with A/C in place of the screens, and a clear timeline so they always knew what came next. Now it’s the room they use every day, all year.

What the investment included
  • Conceptual design, signed & sealed drawings and energy calculation
  • Permit preparation and submission, inspections through final
  • Foundation, walls and roof tied into the existing house
  • Electrical, plumbing and A/C connection per the approved plan
  • Insulation, drywall, paint, trim and flooring
