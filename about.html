Our story

Fabricio Herrera didn’t just learn this trade, he grew up in it. He started at 16 in Ecuador, learning the craft from the ground up, and built and ran several businesses before putting down roots in Florida.

Ana, Fabricio’s wife, has been part of the company from the beginning. While Fabricio was building, she was sitting down with homeowners, helping them picture the finished space before the first wall went up.

Today we live in Longwood with our two daughters, and the company is what it has always been: a family that treats your home the way we treat our own.

Our mission

To treat every project as if it were our own. We work as a single, cohesive team committed to honesty, clear communication, and craftsmanship that lasts.

How we work together

Every project has two halves, and each of us owns one.

The bones

Fabricio

Owner · Licensed Contractor

Fabricio leads the rough construction: structure, framing, roofing, everything behind the walls. These are the parts you’ll never see once the job is done, and the parts that decide whether it lasts.

The finish

Ana

Designer & Drafter

Ana draws your plans and prepares photorealistic renderings so you can see your project before it’s built. She guides you through every finish selection, then supervises the final phase in person: tile, paint and fixtures.

So the person who helped you choose your tile is the same person making sure it’s installed right.

The next generation

Our daughters are growing up in the business. Melina and Domenica work together on our social media, preparing the project photos and posts you see from us.

Away from the job site

When we’re not building, we’re outdoors. Fabricio is a scuba diver and boater, and as a family we travel and spend as much time outside as we can.

The “own home” rule

If a finish or a fix isn’t good enough for our own family home, it isn’t good enough for yours.

The Herrera Standard

How we run this company is simple: we lead with a “behind the work” mindset. To us, the Herrera Standard means that the parts of the project you can’t see—the integrity of the framing, the honesty in the quote, and the respect for your property—are just as important as the finished result.

We aren’t a collection of disconnected contractors; we are a unified team that takes personal responsibility for every nail driven and every tile laid.
